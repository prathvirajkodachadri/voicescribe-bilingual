🎧 How to hear computer sound (Meet / Zoom / YouTube)

  1. Click System Sound (teal button) → Chrome asks “Choose what to share”
  2. Select the Tab where your colleague is speaking (e.g., Google Meet) — best is Chrome Tab
  3. ✅ IMPORTANT: Turn ON “Share system audio” toggle at bottom-left of the share dialog (otherwise we can’t hear)
  4. Click Share → you’ll see green waveform moving when sound plays
  5. Now speak OR let your colleague speak — both appear as EN + JA instantly
  6. To stop, click ⏹️ Stop System Audio

If no waveform: re-click and ensure “Share system audio” is checked. On Mac, you may need to use Chrome → Share Window + BlackHole virtual device. Extension version can capture tab audio directly without this step.
